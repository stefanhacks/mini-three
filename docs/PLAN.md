# WoW: Hantō: Plan

WoW: Hantō is a browser look-alike of the World of Warcraft: Forever profession window, specialised for five professions: Alchemy, Herbalism, Cooking, Fishing and First Aid, always in that order. It lists their recipes and gatherables, and lets the user attach notes and mark recipes as known. It is inspired by the in-game window, not a 1:1 copy, and every piece of UI art is our own.

## Product

- **Five fixed tabs.** There is no way to pick professions. Alchemy and Herbalism (primary) are set apart from Cooking, Fishing and First Aid (secondary) by a gap and a bronze rim.
- **List.** Per tab: gatherables (Herbalism's herbs, Fishing's fish) and recipes grouped by the game's category, sorted by the skill they are learnable at, which also shows beside each name.
- **Detail pane.**
  - Recipes: name, `learn X · yellow Y, grey Z`, the created item's "Use:" text, notes, where it is learned from, reagents.
  - Gatherables: where they are found (zones), the recipes that use them, notes.
- **Search.** Matches names, a recipe's reagents, and a gatherable's zones ("Mulgore" lists Peacebloom, "Barrens" on Fishing lists Deviate Fish).
- **Known recipes and notes** are saved in `localStorage` (`hanto:v1`): one browser, one device. A "Known only" filter narrows the list.
- **Not shown:** recipes learnable above skill 300 (the game doesn't support them yet) and Season of Discovery recipes.
- Desktop shows list and detail side by side with the tabs on the right. Mobile shows the list full screen with a tab strip, and the detail pane slides in over it.
- A blurred background with a few selectable scenes.

## Decisions

- **Data.** Generated by `npm run data` and committed (`src/data/*.json`); Netlify never downloads anything. Sources, pins, rules and how to update: [DATA.md](DATA.md). Only the five professions are read, tested or kept.
- **Server facts.** Fish zones and trainer skill levels come from the CMaNGOS Classic database (GPLv3), and herb zones from QuestieDB (GPLv3). Only derived facts are kept, never their files or coordinates.
- **Icons.** `Item.IconFileDataID` is mapped to an icon name through the community listfile; the browser loads the image from `wow.zamimg.com` (CORS-enabled, so images can be WebGL textures). A missing icon leaves an empty frame tinted by quality.
- **UI art.** Drawn with Pixi `Graphics`, turned into textures once at startup, used through `NineSliceSprite`. No image files, no Blizzard art.
- **Background.** Our own procedural scenes, rendered once and blurred.
- **Font.** Marcellus (SIL OFL), self-hosted, as a free stand-in for Friz Quadrata.
- **Text input.** DOM `<textarea>` (notes) and `<input>` (search) placed over the canvas: native typing, IME, mobile keyboards, accessibility.
- **Dependencies.** `pixi.js` is the only runtime dependency. Animation uses Pixi's ticker, scrolling is custom, CSV, Lua and SQL parsing are hand-written.
- **Saved data.** The `hanto:v1` format is unchanged by the specialisation: the old profession choice is ignored, and notes and known flags for the five professions carry over.

## Risks

- **Server data coverage.** Classic databases don't know Forever-only fish, zones or trainer changes: those show blanks, listed in the data report.
- **Icon names.** The listfile may miss Forever-only icons; those slots keep the empty frame.
- **Hotlinked icons.** If zamimg blocks hotlinking, icons disappear but the app keeps working.
- **Licensing.** Recipe and item data are game facts from the client. The project stays non-commercial, in line with Blizzard's fan content terms.
- **Mobile text input.** The on-screen keyboard resizes the viewport differently on iOS and Android; `visualViewport` handles it.
